# Notes on design decisions
# Olist Dashboard Notes

## Phase 9 — KPI Design

The goal of the dashboard is to summarize Olist's business performance from 2017 to 2018 and provide actionable insights for executive, operations, and marketing teams.

---

## Executive KPIs

### 1. Total Revenue

**Formula:**  
SUM(price + freight_value)

**Business meaning:**  
Measures total delivered order revenue generated by the marketplace.

**Business decision:**  
Used to track overall business growth and marketplace performance.

---

### 2. Total Orders

**Formula:**  
COUNT(DISTINCT order_id)

**Business meaning:**  
Measures total delivered order volume.

**Business decision:**  
Helps determine whether revenue growth is driven by more orders or higher order value.

---

### 3. Average Order Value

**Formula:**  
Total Revenue / Total Orders

**Business meaning:**  
Measures how much customers spend per order on average.

**Business decision:**  
Used to evaluate pricing, product mix, and upsell opportunities.

---

### 4. Total Customers

**Formula:**  
COUNT(DISTINCT customer_unique_id)

**Business meaning:**  
Measures the number of unique customers who placed delivered orders.

**Business decision:**  
Shows marketplace reach and customer acquisition performance.

---

## Operational KPIs

### 5. Average Review Score

**Formula:**  
AVG(review_score)

**Business meaning:**  
Measures customer satisfaction.

**Business decision:**  
Used to monitor service quality and customer experience.

---

### 6. On-Time Delivery Rate

**Formula:**  
% of delivered orders where order_delivered_customer_date <= order_estimated_delivery_date

**Business meaning:**  
Measures logistics performance.

**Business decision:**  
Helps identify whether delivery performance is hurting customer satisfaction.

---

### 7. Average Delivery Days

**Formula:**  
AVG(order_delivered_customer_date - order_purchase_timestamp)

**Business meaning:**  
Measures how long customers wait to receive orders.

**Business decision:**  
Used to evaluate fulfillment speed and logistics efficiency.

---

## Customer and Retention KPIs

### 8. Repeat Purchase Rate

**Formula:**  
% of customers with 2 or more delivered orders

**Business meaning:**  
Measures customer retention behavior.

**Business decision:**  
Helps determine whether the marketplace depends mostly on new customer acquisition or repeat purchases.

---

### 9. RFM Segment Size

**Formula:**  
COUNT(customer_unique_id) by RFM segment

**Business meaning:**  
Shows how many customers belong to segments such as Champions, Potential Loyalists, At Risk, and Lost.

**Business decision:**  
Helps marketing teams target campaigns based on customer value and activity.

---

### 10. Revenue by RFM Segment

**Formula:**  
SUM(monetary) by RFM segment

**Business meaning:**  
Shows which customer segments contribute the most revenue.

**Business decision:**  
Helps prioritize high-value customers for loyalty, upsell, or win-back campaigns.

---

## Main Dashboard Story

The dashboard should answer:

1. How much revenue did Olist generate?
2. How did revenue change over time?
3. Which states and product categories generated the most revenue?
4. How satisfied were customers?
5. Did delivery delays affect reviews?
6. Are customers returning after their first purchase?
7. Which customer segments should marketing prioritize?

---

## Recommended Dashboard Pages

### Page 1 — Executive Overview

Audience: executives, business stakeholders

Recommended visuals:

- KPI cards: Total Revenue, Total Orders, AOV, Total Customers
- Monthly revenue trend
- Revenue by customer state
- Top product categories by revenue
- Review score distribution

---

### Page 2 — Customer Intelligence

Audience: marketing and CRM team

Recommended visuals:

- RFM customer count by segment
- Revenue by RFM segment
- Cohort retention heatmap
- Repeat purchase rate
- Customer segment explanation box

---

### Page 3 — Operations and Customer Satisfaction

Audience: operations/logistics team

Recommended visuals:

- Average review score
- On-time vs late delivery comparison
- Average review score by delivery status
- Delivery delay vs review score
- Average delivery days by state

---

## Key Insights to Communicate

1. Revenue grew strongly throughout 2017 and remained high in 2018.
2. Revenue is concentrated in São Paulo, Rio de Janeiro, and Minas Gerais.
3. Health and beauty, watches and gifts, bed bath table, sports leisure, and computers accessories are major revenue categories.
4. Customer reviews are generally positive, with an average score around 4 out of 5.
5. Late deliveries strongly reduce customer satisfaction.
6. Repeat purchase behavior is very low, which suggests Olist relies heavily on new customer acquisition.
7. RFM segmentation helps identify customers for loyalty, upsell, and win-back campaigns.
